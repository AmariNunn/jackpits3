# Jack Pitts Health Foundation — Full Website Revamp Prompt

## Overview
Revamp the existing Jack Pitts Health Foundation website (jackpits - Replit project) into a visually stunning, story-driven experience that honors Jack Pitts' extraordinary legacy as a football pioneer, civil rights figure, and community health champion. The site should feel like a premium sports foundation meets legacy tribute — think ESPN documentary energy crossed with The Masters aesthetic.

---

## Design Direction

**Aesthetic**: Editorial sports legacy — rich forest green (#1a6b3a) as dominant color, deep cream/ivory (#f5f0e8) as background, warm gold (#c9973a) as accent, and near-black (#0d1f0f) for text. The vibe is "Sports Illustrated Hall of Fame issue meets civil rights museum exhibit."

**Typography**: 
- Display/Headlines: `Playfair Display` (serif, dramatic) for hero titles
- Subheadings: `Bebas Neue` or `Oswald` (bold, athletic) for stats and labels
- Body: `Lora` (elegant serif) for copy
- Import from Google Fonts

**Key Visual Assets to Use**:
- Real photos from the gallery (tournament photos — group shots on course, dinner, trophies)
- Anime-style illustrations of Jack Pitts (green polo, green cap, blue mirrored sunglasses, white mustache) in three poses: trophy raise, golf swing, putting stance, and celebration fist pump — these should be used as featured hero/section artwork throughout the site
- Use the anime "trophy raise" image on the homepage hero
- Use the "swing" image on the Schedule page
- Use the "putting" image in the Registration section
- Use the "celebration" image on the About/Legacy section
